## Software changes

The previous driver cleared its initialized flag after one failed status or
measurement read, permanently stopping acquisition. It also used a 100 kHz
14-byte measurement burst under Wire's millisecond timeout. That provided
little timing margin, while all transport failures collapsed to one error.
The original log did not retain enough detail to prove the exact transport
failure mechanism.

BMM350 now uses 400 kHz transfers, caller-owned buffers and a shared 1800 µs
deadline per transaction, plus the 2 µs Fast-mode bus-free interval. STOP
completion is checked before the next transfer; normal address NACKs do not
unnecessarily reset the peripheral. A failed runtime read invalidates health
immediately and retries with 100–1000 ms backoff. No sensor reset, allocation
or OTP reload runs in the flight loop.

Startup checks every OTP transport/status result, executes Bosch's X/Y
positive/negative self-test, restores 25 Hz/eight-average normal operation,
and verifies configuration registers. The vendor Bosch sources are unchanged.
Nonfinite data, total field above 2000 µT, temperature outside −40…85 °C or
failed self-test responses cannot become accepted magnetic samples.

`MAG_HEALTH`, `MAG_DATA` and `MAG_CHECK` distinguish communication, accepted
measurements, rejected/raw values and startup self-test/configuration results.
The GUI displays these failures explicitly instead of implying that missing
installation calibration is the only problem. See [heading diagnostics and
setup](MAGNETIC_HEADING.md).

## Actual board results

A 30-second capture after the final image started recorded:

```text
MAG_HEALTH,enabled=1,initialized=1,healthy=0,communicating=1,stage=11,result=0,chip_id=51,id14=51,id15=-1,bus_errors=0,last_reg=0,status=0,samples=0,reads=1434,invalid=1434,bus_status=0,consecutive=0,recoveries=0,otp_error=0,self_test=1
MAG_CHECK,x=316.45,y=326.24,err=0,pmu=40,aggr=54,axes=7,st=0
MAG_DATA,x=-3868.37,y=-3356.23,z=-39.29,temp=29.06,raw=-509024/-446675/-959/50558
```

Read counts rose throughout the capture, with no I²C errors. X ranged from
−3868.65 to −3868.03 µT, Y from −3356.66 to −3355.91 µT, and Z from −39.70 to
−38.46 µT. Temperature was 29.04–29.08 °C. Large X/Y values are present in the
raw burst before Bosch compensation and persist after Bosch's magnetic reset
and passing self-test. The configuration and OTP acquisition checks passed.
These observations establish the remaining invalid-data condition; they do
not establish its physical cause. No offsets were invented to hide it.

Both BMI270s and BMP581 were healthy, gyro calibration completed, and
`flight_enabled=1,armed=0,failsafe=1,timing_fault=0` remained reported.
Across 600 output reports, SERVO3 stayed at 1000 µs and reserved SERVO8/9
stayed at 1000 µs. The BMM350 task's maximum observed runtime was 734 µs,
with zero task overruns. No motor or flight test was performed.

Yaw remains `source=GYRO,valid=0,reason=driver_unavailable,configured=0,hold=0`.
Usable field readings and measured installation calibration are still needed
before magnetic yaw/ASSIST heading hold can qualify.
